# Route Optimization AI

**Zion Route Optimization AI** — multi-stop route optimization that respects traffic, time windows, vehicle capacity and cost constraints.

> Part of the **Zion AI App Network** — see [ZION_APP_NETWORK.md](./ZION_APP_NETWORK.md) for the full mesh of interlinked apps.

Live app: https://ziontechgroup.com/route-optimization-ai/

## ✨ Features
- **Multi-stop optimization** — hundreds of stops per vehicle, re-optimized in seconds.
- **Live traffic & windows** — respects delivery windows, driver hours and real-time traffic.
- **Cost-aware routing** — balances fuel, tolls, overtime and vehicle wear.
- **Dynamic re-routing** — absorbs cancellations and urgent orders mid-day.
- **KPI dashboard** — cost per stop, on-time rate, km saved vs. baseline.
